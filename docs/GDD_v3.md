# Game Design Document
**Version 3.0 — Living Document**

**Genre:** Turn-Based RPG  
**Perspective:** Top-Down Pixel Art  
**Core Tech:** AI-Powered Narrative  
**Foundation:** BG3-Inspired Systems

An AI-driven, top-down turn-based pixel art RPG where every conversation rewrites history, every choice expands the world, and no two stories are ever the same. Beneath the narrative, a full simulation of economics, ecology, disease, warfare, law, agriculture, geology, and society runs continuously. The world does not wait for the player. It does not pause.

---

## Overview

This document captures the full design vision for an AI-native RPG with:

- Persistent NPC memory and personality simulation
- AI-generated narrative branching and world expansion
- D20/BG3-inspired tactical turn-based combat
- Full world simulation (economy, ecology, disease, law, politics, warfare)
- Dynamic factions, religion, identity, and social class systems
- Legacy-style multi-generation playthroughs
- AI-constrained stylized pixel-art generation
- Context-sensitive generative music, voices, and combat audio
